## Melee

| Action             | Type                     | Test / modifier                | Key effect                                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All Out Attack     | Full                     | +20 WS                         | Melee only; cannot Dodge or Parry until next turn                    |
| Called Shot        | Full                     | Hard (-20) WS                  | Declare hit location; hit declared location on success               |
| Charge             | Full                     | +10 WS                         | Target must be at least 4 m away; final 4 m must be straight         |
| Guarded Attack     | Full                     | -10 WS; +10 Dodge/Parry        | Melee attack; defence bonus until next turn                          |
| Stun               | Full                     | Hard (-20) WS                  | Compare 1d10+SB vs. 1d10+TB+head AP; stun for difference in rounds   |
| Knock-Down         | Half                     | Opposed Strength; +10 if moved | Winner knocks target Prone                                           |
| Feint              | Half                     | Opposed WS                     | Next Standard Attack against same target cannot be Dodged or Parried |
| Manoeuvre          | Half                     | Opposed WS                     | Move target 1 m; attacker may also move 1 m                          |
| Grapple — initiate | Standard Attack / Charge | WS; unarmed only               | Start Grapple instead of dealing normal damage                       |
| Grapple — target   | Half                     | Strength or Contortionist      | Break Free, Slip Free, or Take Control                               |
| Parry              | Reaction                 | Challenging (+0) WS            | Negates 1 melee hit; requires parry-capable melee weapon             |
